# -*- org -*-

* 0.1 (Q3 2026) (fork of elm-playground's TinySoldat)
# unless mentioned otherwise, everything below was done by Claude Code
# (with direction from me)

** Features
- the other weapons: the knife (a blow, or thrown: it lies where it
  fell), the chainsaw, the LAW (crouched or lying only), chosen as
  the second weapon (the key c, secondary=); empty hands punch
- the bonus kits (bonus=N; none without it, as in Soldat): the Flame
  God and its flamer, the predator, the berserker, the vest, cluster
  grenades (five bits each)
- a budget of lines: make loc (10,000 for src/, opening comments not
  counted), and docs/omitted.md, what of OpenSoldat is not here and
  what it would take
- a Rambomatch (mode=rm), from Soldat's GAMESTYLE_RAMBO: the bow on
  the map, for empty hands; its arrow, which kills at once and stays
  in a wall; Rambo's rules (who may hurt whom, which kills count, its
  health coming back, first to 30); Soldat's bots after the bow; the
  bow's pictures and sounds
- the lobby's screen: on a server without room=, the rooms to choose
  from (one for each map, and the server's others, with their
  players' number), the arrows and enter; escape, in a game, comes
  back to it
- over the network, the weapon to come back with is the player's (the
  keys 1 to 9 and 0, a new message, Weapon), and the interface
  (health, ammo, jets, the menu when dead) is its own soldier's: it
  was the first seat's
- the player's side of a round on a server is tested
  (tests/server/Unit_online.ml: Soldat_online against Soldat_server
  over localhost, 100 ms each way): a seat, its own soldier moved at
  once and never corrected when left alone, corrected when shot, the
  others moving in every frame
- fixed: the jump's sound was never heard (jump.wav was ADPCM: now
  plain samples; a test reads each sound's format); a player's name
  in the scores is its shirt's colour made lighter (a dark blue was
  not read on the sky)
- over the network (docs/plan.md's step 8): mini-soldat-server plays a
  room's round, 60 ticks a second, its soldiers bots' until a player
  takes one (Soldat_room); the game as bytes (Soldat_wire) in two new
  messages (Input, Seat, World); a player's side (Soldat_online, the
  flags server=, nick=, room=): its keys up, the round down, its own
  soldier predicted and the others interpolated (elm-playground's
  Prediction and Interpolation), the room's talk; from a browser too.
  docs/network.md says how, and what of elm-playground it uses
- teams and flags (docs/plan.md's step 7): a soldier's team (its
  colour, its places to appear at, its walls, no harm from its own);
  a round's mode, a deathmatch, a team match or capture the flag, the
  map's own or the flag mode=; the flag, a thing of four points,
  taken, carried, dropped, sent home, captured; Soldat's bots in
  teams; ctf_Ash and its content; the key m for the next map
- heard, and alive (docs/plan.md's step 6). The rules say what a tick
  gave to hear and see as values (Soldat_event); the sparks are made of
  them (Soldat_sparks, from Sparks.pas: shells, clips, blood, chips,
  smoke, an explosion's fire, the jets') and drawn with Soldat's
  pictures (Soldat_sparks_view), an explosion its 16 in place of the
  disc, shaking the camera; the sounds (Soldat_sfx: 82 of Soldat's
  recordings in data/sfx) are played by where they are
  (Soldat_sound, from Sound.pas: quieter with the distance, to a side,
  a far fight a rumble, the jets a loop). 8-bit recordings rewritten
  as 16-bit
- flags mute and sparks=N; Gen_assets' third word, half
- a deathmatch as Soldat's (docs/plan.md's step 5). Soldat_bots, from
  AI.pas: Soldat's own bots, along the map's waypoints when they see
  nobody, by SimpleDecision's ladder of distances when they do, each
  with the character of one of Soldat's 16 .bot files (data/bots/,
  carried in the program). Soldat_things, from Things.pas: a weapon
  thrown away (the key f) or dropped by the dead, falling, lying
  still, picked up by empty hands; medikits and grenade kits where
  the map puts them. The first to 10 kills or the best after 10
  minutes; the dead back at a place taken by chance; the scores
  ranked, the time left
- flags bots=N (3) and waypoints (the bots' paths drawn)
- of TinySoldat's bots, the one on elm-playground's Sense and Bot is
  kept as an example of that library (Soldat_engine_bot): the flag
  ai=engine puts it in a round in place of the last of Soldat's. The
  one written by hand went
- Soldat's weapons (docs/plan.md's step 4). Soldat_weapons: its own
  weapons.ini read, the ten primaries, the USSOCOM, the grenade.
  Soldat_soldier, from ControlSprite, TSprite.Fire and ThrowGrenade: a
  weapon in the hands and one on the back; the trigger, held or once a
  pull; the clip, its reload and their animations; the shotgun's
  shells; the Barrett's and the minigun's wait; the scatter by how one
  moves and stands; the two Eagles; the six pellets and their kick;
  the recoil; the change of weapon; a grenade thrown harder the longer
  held. Soldat_bullets, from Bullets.pas: a bullet against the map
  (the ricochet), the colliders and the soldiers' seven circles;
  damage as speed x Damage x the place's; the push; through a body;
  weaker with the distance; a grenade bouncing; the explosion.
  Soldat_ragdoll: the sticks a hard death cuts
- the keys r (reload), q (the other weapon), e (a grenade), 1 to 9 and
  0 (the weapon to appear with; the flag weapon=N)
- the weapons drawn: in the hands with clip and muzzle's fire, on the
  back; a grenade; an explosion. Their pictures are files
  (data/weapons-gfx, asked through Soldat_assets)
- an interface: health, ammunition, jets, grenades; Soldat's menu of
  weapons on the title and while dead
- the scatter from a seed in the round's state (Lehmer), not Random: a
  round replays the same
- everything TinySoldat had in elm-playground 0.3.3: one arena of
  polygons, three soldiers (you and two bots) running, jumping and
  flying on their jets, bullets swept against the map and the
  soldiers, bouncing grenades and their blasts, the dead as ragdolls
  (particles and sticks), respawning, the first to 5 kills; the bots
  by hand or, with ai=engine, on Sense and Bot; the flag hitboxes
- renamed MiniSoldat (the title screen, the help)
- the map looks as Soldat's does: its texture on its polygons, shaded
  by their corners, and its scenery in its three layers, drawn by the
  game itself into tiles (Soldat_raster: textured Gouraud triangles,
  sprites placed as MapGraphics.pas places a prop; Soldat_scene: tiles
  of 256 units made as the camera comes near, the flat colours until
  then)
- Soldat's content as files (Soldat_assets): from data/, or any folder
  laid out as Soldat's (base=, e.g. a checkout of opensoldat-base:
  every map with its texture and scenery, map=ctf_Ash by name), or
  beside the page in a browser; nothing waits for a file
- a .bmp reader (Bmp), for the scenery never redrawn as PNG
- the key g and the flag graphics=N: the game as each step drew it (1
  skeletons on flat colours, 2 the soldiers' pictures, 3 the map's
  texture and scenery)
- deadly, hurting, healing and exploding polygons act on a soldier's
  health (HandleSpecialPolyTypes); the map's colliders stop bullets
- in a browser: the pictures fetched as plain pixels (Gen_assets at
  'make website': elm-playground's PNG decoder is quadratic compiled
  to JavaScript). And a change to elm-playground, this game's first:
  its web platform kept the last 32 pictures, the textured map and
  three soldiers are 64, and every one was encoded again each frame
  (9 frames a second); it keeps them all now (60)
- docs/architecture.md: the program's shape, a frame's way to the
  screen on each backend, what each layer keeps;
  scripts/perf/bitmap_toy, the toy that shows a backend's limit;
  scripts/perf/web_probe.js, a page timed in a real browser
- the soldier looks as Soldat's does (Soldat_gostek, adapted from
  GostekGraphics.pas and its table): 16 parts and a helmet, each one
  of Soldat's pictures hung between two points of the skeleton,
  turned along them, the thighs and the forearms stretching, mirrored
  facing left, tinted the shirt's, the trousers' and the skin's
  colours; the jets' feet; the pistol in the hands; dead, the same on
  the skeleton let loose
- its head and its hands turn to the cursor (Sprites.pas's rotation of
  points 12, 15 and 19); a bullet leaves from the hand
- the soldier's pictures packed into the program at build time, as
  their pixels (Gen_pictures: 82 KB), each tinted and turned over once
  when first needed
- flag sticks: the skeletons drawn over the soldiers
- the soldier moves as Soldat's does (Soldat_soldier, adapted from
  Sprites.pas and Control.pas): one particle stepped by Soldat's Euler
  (gravity 0.06 a tick, a speed kept at 0.99), the keys as forces and
  animations in ControlSprite's order and with its constants (a run,
  a jump given during its animation's frames 9 to 14, a jump sideways,
  a crouch and a crouched walk, lying down, crawling and getting up,
  a roll, a backflip, the jets and their fuel), the collision with
  the map by points in one sector (the head, the feet, a circle, the
  corners) with its friction rules, ice and bouncy polygons. Soldat's
  keys: a/d, w, s, x, the mouse's two buttons
- Soldat's 44 animations and the gostek's skeleton: their files read
  (Poa), packed into the program at build time (Gen_anims: 1.2 MB of
  text as 180 KB of singles), each with its speed and its loop
  (Soldat_anims)
- the game is in Soldat's units and coordinates: a tick a frame, a
  soldier 20 tall, y downwards; the picture alone turns y over
- a soldier drawn as its skeleton's sticks; dead, that skeleton let
  loose (Particles.step and relax with Soldat's damping and gravity,
  CheckSkeletonMapCollision)
- Soldat's camera: 0.14 of the way to the player a tick, moved by a
  seventh of the mouse; 640 units across
- the map's own sectors: a point is tested against its sector's walls
  alone, by their perps, as PolyMap.pas does. A tick on Arena2: 0.02
  ms, from 2.6; on the biggest map 0.1, from 12.5
- the toy's gun with the USSOCOM's numbers (speed, damage by the place
  hit, push, fire interval), a bullet tested along its way against the
  sector's walls and 7 circles of a soldier's skeleton; Soldat's 90
  ticks without fire or hits after appearing, its health of 150, its
  3 seconds before coming back; the soldiers appear far apart
- gone with the toy's soldier: the rigid-body world, the map made
  twice as big, the grenades (until the weapons), map=toy
- Soldat's own maps: a .pms file read (Pms, after MapFile.pas: the
  polygons and their kinds, the sectors, the props, the colliders, the
  spawn points, the waypoints) and made into the game's map
  (Soldat_map.of_pms: twice as big, y turned over, the triangles
  counterclockwise, which kinds stop a soldier and which a bullet, the
  sky's two colours, the polygons in flat colours, the transparent
  ones not drawn). All of Soldat's 99 maps are read and played on
- Arena2 carried in the program (base64, as elm-playground embeds its
  files), the map the game starts on, in a browser too; map=FILE any
  other, map=toy TinySoldat's screen
- a camera following the player, towards where it aims (Camera2d),
  never beyond the map; the score stays on the screen
- drawn in Soldat's order: the background, the soldiers and what
  flies, then the map's polygons over them
- a soldier fallen out of the map dies
- in a browser: the same game compiled to JavaScript by js_of_ocaml,
  on the Playground's web platform (src/main/web; make serve)
- a website, docs/, for Github Pages: a page written by hand (what it
  is, how to play, that it is generated by an AI, the credits to
  Soldat's author) and the game to play (make website)
- mini-soldat-server: a lobby over WebSocket, players naming
  themselves, entering rooms and talking; the games in the rooms to
  come. Soldat_protocol (the messages as bytes, on elm-playground's
  Wire), Soldat_lobby (who is in which room, a value), Soldat_server
  (the sockets, on elm-playground's Server)

** build infra
- one opam package, mini-soldat, standing on elm-playground's packages
  (0.3.3): the Playground for its window, drawing and Physics layer,
  tiny_libs for the 2D physics and the bots' senses
- two programs: mini-soldat (Cairo's platform when it is installed,
  else the software one) and mini-soldat-software (the Playground's
  own rasterizer, SDL only); a third in a browser (elm_playground_web,
  js_of_ocaml), and the server
- make all is 'dune build @default', so that the web program's page is
  copied beside it; .dockerignore (_build)
- ./configure (after mini-chrome's): checks for SDL2 and Cairo
  (--software: no Cairo), finds elm-playground's packages (from opam,
  or a 'make install' in an elm-playground checkout, or else pinned
  from the 0.3.3 tag)
- bin/ symlink to _build/install/default/bin: ./bin/mini-soldat

** test infra
- the tests of what was copied, from elm-playground's
  tests/games/Unit_games.ml: tests/game (3), the game's libraries
  linked as they are (no copy of the source with its main taken out)
- Dockerfile and docker.yml Github Actions CI check (OCaml 4.14.4 and
  5.5.1), make build-docker
- tests/game (17): the soldier's moves on a floor made by hand,
  against numbers worked out from Soldat's constants (a fall's top
  speed 5.94, a run's 2.9, a jump's 8 ticks of wind-up and 85 of
  height, a crouched walk's 1.0...); a round replays the same; six
  bullets in the head
- tests/anim (8): the files' reader, the 44 animations' frames, how
  one advances
- tests/render (14): where a part's picture goes, worked out by hand
  from Soldat's arithmetic; a picture tinted and turned over; a
  triangle and a sprite drawn into pixels
- tests/assets (6): a .bmp written by hand, a picture as pixels and
  back
- tests/map (8): Arena2 read from the bytes carried, what is in it,
  what is refused; the game's map made of it: its perps, its sectors
  (which list a wall where its outline is, not its inside); what
  stops what
- scripts/perf/Frame_bench: a frame's time without drawing, on the
  toy and Arena2, or on any .pms files named (all 99: 0.7 to 12.5 ms)
- tests/server (9): the protocol's worked example and what it refuses,
  the lobby's rule called as a function, and the server over localhost
  with real WebSocket clients (elm-playground's Relay_client)

** internals
- TinySoldat.ml's one file cut along its own sections into libraries,
  after Soldat's shared/ and client/: src/map (Soldat_map), src/game
  (Soldat_model, Soldat_ragdoll, Soldat_bots, Soldat_update),
  src/render (Soldat_view), src/main (MiniSoldat.ml); the frames it
  draws are TinySoldat's, pixel for pixel
- docs/opensoldat.md: Soldat's sources (MIT) and content (CC BY 4.0),
  what is in them, and which of its files each module stands for
- data/: Soldat's content copied here, CC BY 4.0 (data/README.md)
- LICENSE-OpenSoldat.md: the MIT notice of the Pascal the soldier is
  adapted from
- Soldat_update.tick takes the player's intent and where it looks, and
  knows no keyboard and no screen: what a test calls, and a server
  will
- the map is a value the round carries (Soldat_map.t), no longer
  constants of a module
- docs/notes_soldat_in_playground.md: Soldat's terms, ideas and code
  next to what each is in the Playground, from reading both sides;
  docs/plan.md: the steps from the toy's soldiers to Soldat
- docs/network.md: playing over the network, what is there (the
  lobby) and what is to come (the game on the server, snapshots)
